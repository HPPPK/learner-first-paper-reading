# Curated examples

These examples show the public behavior contract in three different routes. They
are compact, edited demonstrations—not transcripts of completed learning studies
and not evidence that every agent host will behave identically.

| Example | Route | What to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Guided DLinear reading](dlinear-guided-reading.md) | Guided learning | Opening contract, route agreement, minimal scaffold, original-paper assignment |
| [Focused equation explanation](focused-equation.md) | Direct explanation | Mechanism, dimensions, worked example, boundary |
| [Reproduction evidence](reproduction-evidence.md) | Implementation/reproduction | Reported state-3 evidence and the six-state boundary |

Every example labels **Paper**, **Background**, **Analyst**, and **Unknown**.
Those labels show provenance; they do not certify that the underlying source or
execution was independently inspected outside the conditions stated in the file.

To evaluate the skill itself, use the tracked [behavior scenarios](../tests/scenarios.md)
and read the [evaluation limits](../tests/evaluation-results.md).
