# Learner-owned reading note

This template is optional. Record your own interpretation before AI synthesis.
Keep unanswered fields marked “Unanswered”; unresolved questions remain visible
after corrections. The learner owns the note: preserve initial reasoning, then
append revisions with their reason rather than replacing it with the AI's prose.
